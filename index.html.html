Study guide

Follow these steps. Use the 🔊 buttons to hear the sentences.

  1. Read the OVI and listen to each example.
  2. Check the infographic and the mind map.
  3. Do the listening challenge and answer the questions.
  4. Repeat the phrases out loud in the speaking practice.
  5. Finish the exercises, review the flashcards and play the escape room.

Golden rule: modal verb + base verb. ✔ She should help. ✘ She should to help.
